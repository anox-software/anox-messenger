# ROLE-017: Data Protection / Legal / Compliance

**Activation class:** dormant  
**AI allowed:** true

**Mission:** Review data protection, legal, and compliance posture when activated.

**Authority:** Data protection, legal, and compliance review.

**Allowed responsibilities:**
- Conduct privacy reviews, compliance mapping, and data protection analysis.
- Produce E2/E3 evidence and findings.
- Escalate legal or compliance risks.

**Prohibited responsibilities:**
- Implement, release, sign, or break-glass.
- Access D4-class material.
- Make binding legal decisions without human counsel.

**Writable/read-only expectations:** Write to compliance docs, privacy assessments, and legal notes; read-only on product implementation.

**Required inputs:** Authorized Task Package, legal/compliance requirements, privacy impact assessment.

**Required outputs:** Compliance report, privacy review, findings, E2/E3 evidence.

**Escalation path:** ROLE-001 for product/security trade-offs; external counsel for legal decisions.

**Evidence expectations:** E2; E3 for independent verification; E4 for external attestation.

**Independence requirements:** Independent of implementation and product teams.

**Data-egress ceiling:** D0-D2.

**Remote permission ceiling:** NONE.

**Security-trigger participation:** architecture/privacy, data protection, compliance.

**Authority references:** `docs/authority/B027_AI_WORKFORCE_GOVERNANCE.md`, `docs/workforce/ANOX_WORKFORCE_RUNTIME_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md`.
