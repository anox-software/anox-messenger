# B-020 — Full Product + UX
**Status:** FROZEN v1.1

V1 product surface includes onboarding/registration/license, no-recovery warning, username/contact addressing, chats, contact requests, official Support relationship, 1:1 E2EE text, attachments, SAS verification/security-change UX, delivery/read states, privacy-first notifications, contact export/import, app lock/screen protection, license renewal, logout, local wipe, account deletion and update UX.

Read receipts default OFF. No typing/global online/last seen. Verification state must be visible; KEY_CHANGED is blocking and cannot be dismissed into trust. Contact QR must not be labeled “verified”. Push notification does not show sender/message content by default.

V1 excludes groups, calls, multi-device, recovery, reactions, message editing, delete-for-everyone, cloud history and support multi-device HA. Username change is not a V1 feature; username immutable after activation.

Official Support uses the same E2EE protocol. “Official” product labeling is not cryptographic verification. Support device loss creates a new support account/identity; old verification does not transfer silently.

Marketing/UX must not say unhackable, zero metadata, fully anonymous, GrapheneOS-certified, OWASP-certified or other unsupported absolute certification claims.
